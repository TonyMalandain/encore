# Who touches the product

Four roles. In a household, one person may hold two of them — that does not
merge the roles, it just means one person carries both sets of expectations.

## Person at the terminal
- **Who they are:** anyone sitting in front of a converted machine. In the
  author's household, a child. They have their own credentials on the machine
  they are connecting to, and no privileges on the terminal itself.
- **What they want:** to sit down, see a login screen, sign in, and use a
  computer. They do not know or care that the machine in front of them is not
  the one doing the work.
- **What would make them give up:** a black screen, a machine that asks them
  to fix something, or sound that does not come out of the speakers in front
  of them.

## Terminal administrator
- **Who they are:** the person with root on the old machine. Installs the
  product, points it at a machine to connect to, and turns the capability on
  and off. Reaches the machine over a text console or remotely, because once
  the capability is active the screen belongs to the remote session.
- **What they want:** to convert a machine in a few minutes, and to be able to
  undo it and get the old machine back unchanged.
- **What would make them give up:** an install that rewrites parts of the
  system they did not agree to, or an off-switch that leaves the machine in a
  state they have to repair by hand.

## Owner of the machine being connected to
- **Who they are:** whoever owns the machine that hosts everybody's sessions.
  In the author's household, the author. The product does nothing to their
  machine and makes no claim about it.
- **What they want:** several people working at once without their own session
  or their own room being invaded — no terminal's audio arriving through their
  speakers.
- **What would make them give up:** discovering that terminals reach into
  their machine in ways they did not agree to. This role never files a
  complaint; it just unplugs things.

## Prospective adopter
- **Who they are:** a stranger who found the project and is deciding, in a
  couple of minutes, whether it is worth their evening. They have not
  installed anything.
- **What they want:** to know within a minute whether their old machine
  qualifies, what will change on it, and how to undo it.
- **What would make them give up:** not being able to tell what the thing
  actually does, or not being able to tell whether it will survive on their
  hardware.
